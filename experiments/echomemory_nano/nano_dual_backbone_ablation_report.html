EchoMemory Dual-Backbone Ablation

This ablation tests the exact research claim behind the new CVPR-shaped method line: a temporal tree and a relation graph solve different failure modes, and the combined backbone is more stable than either alone.

Cases4
Tree-only1
Graph-only3
Dual4

Interpretation

Tree-only

Usually strongest on chronology-heavy queries, but weaker on relation and visual grounding.

Graph-only

Usually strongest on entity/event/image traversal, but weaker on chronology organization.

Dual-backbone

Uses the planner to choose a primary backbone and supplements with the other one.
